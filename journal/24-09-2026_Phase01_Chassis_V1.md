# Phase 01 Chassis V1 Design

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Phase:** Phase 01 - Mobile Platform
**Topic:** Initial chassis design baseline

## Decision

Chassis V1 is accepted as the first mechanical design baseline for Phase 01.

## Reason

Phase 00 bench validation is complete, allowing development to move from subsystem validation to physical mobile-platform integration. A versioned chassis baseline is required before component placement, mounting, wiring, and assembly can be evaluated.

## Result

- The first ARGOS chassis was designed in Fusion 360.
- The editable `.f3d` source and `.dwg` export were archived.
- Phase 01 is now active.
- Mechanical fit, clearances, mounting points, and manufacturability remain to be validated before fabrication or final assembly.

## Files

- `hardware/mechanical/chassis/v1/ARGOS_Chassis_V1.f3d`
- `hardware/mechanical/chassis/v1/ARGOS_Chassis_V1.dwg`
- `hardware/mechanical/chassis/v1/README.md`

## Next Actions

- Review component dimensions and mounting locations.
- Check motor, wheel, sensor, battery, and electronics clearances.
- Plan cable routing and service access.
- Revise the chassis if the fit review identifies conflicts.
- Begin physical platform assembly after the design review.
